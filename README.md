## `03` Featured work

### PIX: a photobooth in the browser

A photobooth with three modes: **solo**, **together** (two people, one device), and **long-distance**. In long-distance mode, two devices share live peer-to-peer video and a server-timed countdown, then get one photo strip with both people in every frame.

`React` `TypeScript` `Vite` `Tailwind CSS` `WebRTC` `Bun`

[Repository](https://github.com/rinerinegoaway/pix-photobooth) · [Live demo](https://pix-photobooth.vercel.app)

### BILIDITO: buy nearby, sell safely

A verified, location-based buy-and-sell marketplace for local communities, starting in Cagayan Province, Philippines. It's a responsive, PWA-ready web app with maps from OpenStreetMap.

`SvelteKit` `Svelte 5` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `PostgreSQL` `Drizzle ORM` `Better Auth` `Leaflet`

[Repository](https://github.com/rinerinegoaway/BILIDITO)
